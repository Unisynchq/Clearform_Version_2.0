# Archetype: Academic research / surveys

## Detection signals

- Intro mentions survey, research, study, course, professor, student, IRB, consent
- Likert or rating scales, demographic blocks, consent screens
- Long-form open responses about experiences or opinions

## Branching guidance

- Keep flows linear unless skip logic is clearly needed for optional sections.
- Use showIf for follow-ups tied to specific scale bands when present.

## Quality expectations

- Coach toward **usable research data** — concrete experience, opinions, what worked or didn't.
- Accept brief but valid demographic answers without length penalties.
- On "describe your experience": multi-sentence answers naming product/research specifics → **green**, not amber for meta-reflection.
- Banned coaching: "informed your understanding or actions", recap "you mentioned…" openings.

## Insights focus

- Report themes without inferring researcher intent.
- Highlight incomplete sections and low completion on consent or demographic blocks.
