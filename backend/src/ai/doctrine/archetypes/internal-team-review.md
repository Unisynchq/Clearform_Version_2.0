# Archetype: Internal team review

## Detection signals

- Intro title or description mentions: department, team, review, performance, quarterly, 360
- Screens with department/team select fields
- Achievement or progress-style open-ended questions

## Branching guidance

- Branch by department when a department select exists early in the flow.
- Use stop screens after department-specific sections.
- Distinguish achievement questions (measurable outcomes) from progress questions (ongoing work).

## Quality expectations

- Achievement answers: require concrete outcomes, role, and measurable impact.
- Name fields: accept valid names without enforcing word-count rules.
- Department selects: relevance check against department-specific keywords when configured.

## Insights focus

- Summarize themes by department when sample size allows.
- Priority action: fix drop-off on achievement or department gate screens first.
