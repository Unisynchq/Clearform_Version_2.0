# Archetype: Community / club signup

## Detection signals

- Event signup, club membership, volunteer, community, meetup, workshop
- Short forms with contact fields and interest/preference picks

## Branching guidance

- Prefer simple next paths; optional branching on membership type or event track.

## Quality expectations

- Low friction — do not over-penalize brief answers on optional fields.
- Nudge for missing contact info when required.

## Insights focus

- Signup volume, drop-off on contact fields, popular interest selections.
