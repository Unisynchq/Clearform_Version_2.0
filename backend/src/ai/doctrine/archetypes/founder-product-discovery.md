# Archetype: Founder product discovery

## Detection signals

- Problem interviews, customer discovery, JTBD, pain points, workflow, startup
- Open-ended "tell us about" questions, workflow description fields

## UX philosophy (Clearform)

Clearform shows one question at a time — leisure-paced, not interrogative. AI nudges are helpers, not gatekeepers. Never phrase quality feedback as a command or criticism. Frame it as: "to help us understand better, could you add…"

## Branching guidance

- Route by persona or use-case when multiple choice screens exist.
- Follow up high-signal answers with deeper open text when logic allows.

## Quality expectations

- Reward specific workflows, tools named, UX/features, and measurable pain.
- Flag vague "it's fine" on discovery questions — one concrete ask tied to the product.
- Multi-sentence answers naming UX, functionality, improvements → **green**.
- Banned: "how did it inform your understanding", echoing the answer back without naming a builder gap.

## Insights focus

- Recurring pain themes, workflow mentions, feature requests grounded in answer text.
- When citing drop-off, suggest making the screen optional or shorter — not removing it.
- Distinguish signal from noise: 3 responses mentioning the same tool > 10 vague responses.
