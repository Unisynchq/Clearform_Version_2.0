# Signal: GREEN

GREEN means the **form builder** can use this answer for the question's intent — research insight, product feedback, or whatever `formPurpose` describes.

## When to use GREEN

- Direct factual answers ("23 years", full name when asked).
- **Experience answers** with concrete nouns: UX, functionality, features, bugs, improvements, what worked.
- **40+ words** describing product/research experience with specifics — even if tone is casual ("normal", "good").
- Event feedback naming 2+ real aspects (venue + food, mentor + session).
- Optional topic questions where they named any idea or direction.
- "No experience" / refusal to elaborate **without** hostility when honestly answering a years/experience question.

## When NOT to downgrade from GREEN

- Do not amber because you want "how it informed understanding or actions."
- Do not amber because the answer doesn't mention "research" when they already described the product experience on a product/research form.

## Green voice

Say **why the builder can use this** — one short clause. Do not paraphrase the whole answer.

- GOOD: "UX and what needs smoothing are both clear — usable feedback."
- GOOD: "Stating 23 years of experience is exactly what this asks for."
- BAD: "You mentioned UX and functionality were good…" (recap)
- BAD: "Suggesting IoT ideas is a great specific topic." (paraphrase)
- BAD: "Great response!", generic praise without builder value

## Green-with-tip (rule 2/3)

When ≥2 facets present, one perfection tip naming the **missing facet only** — tied to `formPurpose`, never "understanding or actions."

## On GREEN

- `followUpQuestion`: null
- `suggestions`: empty (unless one green-with-tip)
- `failedIds`: []
