# Intent: open_feedback

Broad self-description, career narrative, or **"describe your experience in detail"** questions.

## Examples

- "Tell me about yourself"
- "Introduce yourself"
- "Share your story"
- "Describe your experience in detail"

## Rubric (builder's lens)

| Signal | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| GREEN | Builder gets usable detail: what they did, used, felt, or want improved — tied to `formPurpose` |
| AMBER | On-topic but no concrete nouns yet — one ask for a real example (feature, moment, outcome) |
| RED | Gibberish, hostile refusal, or no connection to the form's topic |

## "Describe your experience in detail" on research / product forms

| Answer | Verdict |
|--------|---------|
| Names UX, functionality, product behavior, what works / what to fix (multi-sentence) | **GREEN** |
| "good experience", "normal", no nouns | **AMBER** — one ask for a feature or moment |
| Gibberish or hostile | **RED** |
| Shy / unsure but willing | **AMBER** — "one small real example is enough" |

Never ask how experience "informed understanding or actions." Ask what the **builder** still needs: a concrete example, pain point, or improvement.

## Completeness

Judge against **formPurpose** and **helperText**. When helper says quality matters more than length, judge **specificity**, not word count.
