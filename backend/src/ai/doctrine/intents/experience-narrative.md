# Intent: experience_narrative

Product, service, event, or **form-filling** feedback — NOT job-application work history.

## Examples

- "How was your onboarding experience?"
- "How is your experience with filling this form?"
- "What could we improve about the hackathon?"
- "How did the product make you feel?"

## NOT this intent

- "How many years of relevant experience?" → factual_short
- "Years of experience in this category" → factual_short

## Rubric

| Signal | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| GREEN | Specific and actionable: what happened, where, outcome; or evaluative + concrete noun/step |
| AMBER | Evaluative one-liner ("It was confusing") — ask which step/feature |
| RED | Off-topic, gibberish, repetition |

## Brevity helpers

When helper says "Share as much or as little as you'd like", short evaluative answers are amber at worst, never red for length.

**When the answer is already long and concrete** (e.g. venue + food + positive tone, 15+ words), prefer **GREEN** or **green-with-tip** — not amber. Brevity helpers do not mean "reject detail."

## Multi-part questions (experience + "liked most")

When the label asks for experience **and** what they liked most:

| Answer | Verdict |
|--------|---------|
| "It was good" / "super confusing" only | AMBER — quote tone; ask which moment |
| Venue, food, sessions, mentors named (2+ concrete nouns) | **GREEN** — experience is answered |
| Lists likes but never picks a "most" | **GREEN** + one tip: "Name the single thing you liked most." |
| Explicit favorite named | GREEN — affirm |

Never use generic "anchor to one real thing" copy when venue, food, hackathon, or event details are already in the answer.

## Coaching examples

- "It was confusing." → "Which step or screen felt confusing?"
- "It was good." → "Which part of the product stood out?"
- UX + functionality + improvements named (40+ words) → **GREEN**: "UX and fixes are clear — that's usable."
- "I liked the venue tour and the food during the hackathon." → GREEN: "Venue and food both come through."
- Hostile refusal → RED: skip insults; one honest product detail or none.

Banned: "how did UX inform your understanding", "you mentioned …" recap openings.
