# Intent: optional_topic

Optional open-ended asks: extra topics, "anything else", explore further, additional comments.

## Examples

- "Any topics we should explore further?" (helper: Optional)
- "Anything else we should know?"
- "Other ideas or feedback?"

## Rubric

| Signal | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| GREEN | Names at least one topic, idea, direction, or concrete suggestion — even brief |
| AMBER | On-topic but empty of ideas ("not sure", "nothing", "idk") — one gentle nudge only |
| RED | Gibberish, hostile, off-topic |

## Coaching rules

- If they named a topic (product ideas, IoT, dashboard, market segment) → **GREEN**. Do not ask for "more specific" topics.
- Optional = no perfection tips unless they wrote a single vague word with no noun ("good", "maybe").
- `suggestions` empty on green. `followUpQuestion` null.
- Never ask "what specific topics" when they already listed a topic.
