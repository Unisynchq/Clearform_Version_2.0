# Intent: achievement

Professional accomplishments, impact, measurable outcomes.

## Rubric

| Signal | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| GREEN | Achievement with measurable impact and their role; or 2/3 facets present (green-with-tip) |
| AMBER | Thin story without numbers or role when helper asks for impact |
| RED | Repetition, off-topic, gibberish |

## Facets (from helper)

- measurable impact
- your specific contribution
- what you achieved

## Coaching

- Quote their words. Name whether numbers or outcomes are missing.
- Never ask for an example they already gave.
